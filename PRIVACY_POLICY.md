## 5. Data Security

### 5.1 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security:

- **Encryption:** TLS/SSL for data in transit, AES-256 for data at rest
- **Authentication:** OAuth 2.0, secure token storage
- **Access Control:** Role-based permissions, least privilege principle
- **Monitoring:** Real-time security monitoring and intrusion detection
- **Backups:** Daily encrypted backups with disaster recovery

### 5.2 Password Security

- Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with salt
- We never store plain-text passwords
- We recommend strong, unique passwords

### 5.3 Limitations

While we implement strong security, no system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

## 6. Your Privacy Rights

### 6.1 GDPR Rights (EU Residents)

If you're in the EU, you have:
- **Right to Access:** Request a copy of your data
- **Right to Rectification:** Correct inaccurate data
- **Right to Erasure:** Request deletion of your data
- **Right to Restrict Processing:** Limit how we use your data
- **Right to Data Portability:** Export your data
- **Right to Object:** Opt-out of processing
- **Right to Lodge a Complaint:** Contact your data protection authority

### 6.2 CCPA Rights (California Residents)

If you're in California, you have:
- **Right to Know:** What personal information we collect
- **Right to Delete:** Request deletion of your data
- **Right to Opt-Out:** Opt-out of data sales
- **Right to Non-Discrimination:** No penalty for exercising rights

### 6.3 Other Jurisdictions

We comply with applicable privacy laws in your jurisdiction.
