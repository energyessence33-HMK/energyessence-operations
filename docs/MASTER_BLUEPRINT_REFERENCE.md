## Non-negotiable governance

The application must not claim divine omniscience, prophetic certainty, supernatural guarantees, or scientifically unsupported frequency effects. Scripture, interpretation, design metaphor, AI inference, and verified technical facts are distinct information classes. AI recommendations remain recommendations, while L3 external actions and L4 production, financial, permission, or destructive actions require appropriate human approval and auditable records.

## Controlled implementation sequence

The initial implementation order is: reconcile the authoritative backend, establish the integration gateway and credential boundaries, add approval/execution/audit models, then verify GitHub, Slack, Notion, Zapier MCP, commerce, and specialist workers incrementally. Each phase must remain reviewable, testable, and reversible where feasible.
